Tokens and Test Mode

One quiz token runs one live quiz. Test Mode lets you host the first 5 questions of any quiz for free. A token is used when a live quiz starts; if the connection drops, Reconnect lets you carry on without using another token.

Weekly plans

  • Plans are auto-renewing weekly subscriptions bought through the App Store: Weekly Quiz (1 token a week), Regular (3 a week) and Every Night (7 a week). Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm. A plan renews each week unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current week. Your Apple ID is charged for the next week within 24 hours of renewal.
  • Manage or cancel in the app (Plans & Tokens → Manage plan) or in your Apple ID settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; the current week continues until it ends.
  • Each renewal adds that plan’s tokens. Unused tokens do not expire, including after you cancel.
  • Single tokens can also be bought as one-off purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple. If Apple refunds a purchase, the tokens it added are removed from your balance.

Availability

We work to keep the service running, but a quiz night depends on your internet connection and devices. Rehearse in Test Mode before a big night.
