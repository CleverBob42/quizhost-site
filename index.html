How a night works

Set up in minutes. Bob does the talking.

  1. Pick a quiz

    Choose one of yours or a ready-made quiz, and set your venue.

  2. Put it on the TV

    AirPlay or HDMI to the venue screen. Teams scan the code with the free player app.

  3. Bob runs the show

    Questions are read out, timed and revealed automatically. Pause or skip whenever you like.

  4. Crown the winners

    Round scores, a final leaderboard and confetti for the winning team.
